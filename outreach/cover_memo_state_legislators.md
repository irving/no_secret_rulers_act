Cover Memo for State Legislators

Subject: Voluntary Adoption of a State-Level "No Secret Rulers Act"

Purpose: This memo accompanies the Appendix of the federal No Secret Rulers Act (NSRA). It is intended to help state legislators understand the purpose of the model provisions and how they can be adapted at the state level.

Background: The NSRA addresses the concealment of incapacity among federal officials. However, state officials—governors, legislators, judges, and other statewide officers—also hold powers that can be abused if exercised in secret during periods of incapacity. The federal government cannot and should not mandate how states handle such situations. Instead, the Act provides voluntary model language for states that wish to adopt parallel safeguards.

Key Features of the Model Language:

Mandatory Reporting: Ensures incapacity cannot be hidden by staff or insiders.

Temporary vs. Permanent Action: Distinguishes between recoverable incapacity (where acting officers may serve temporarily) and permanent incapacity (where vacancies must be declared).

Fraud Safeguards: Explicitly criminalizes concealment while continuing to draw salary and benefits.

Public Disclosure: Requires that the fact of incapacity and succession be made public, while protecting medical privacy.

Respect for State Constitutions: Leaves succession procedures and removal mechanisms to existing state constitutional provisions.

Why States Should Consider Adoption:

Promotes transparency and accountability in state government.

Prevents unelected staff from exercising power in secret.

Provides public confidence during crises where an official’s health is in question.

Creates consistency with federal standards, reducing uncertainty in moments of shared state-federal governance.

Next Steps:
State legislatures are encouraged to review the attached Appendix and adapt the provisions as appropriate to their own constitutional frameworks. No coercion or funding mechanism is involved; adoption is entirely voluntary.
